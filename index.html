Augmented reality · pigment archaeology

Prime Form
every colour was a thing first

Point the camera at anything. The colour is read off the surface, matched to the historical pigment it most resembles, and the raw material that pigment was ground from appears — at real specimen scale, on your floor.

A blue mug becomes a lump of Afghan lapis. A terracotta pot becomes the ochre that has been dug for a hundred thousand years.
